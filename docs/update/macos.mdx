---
title: "Update on macOS"
description: "How to update Astro on macOS"
---

Astro does not update itself on macOS. Automatic update checks are off, and **Astro > Check for Updates…** does not install a new version of Astro. To update, download the new version and replace the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your version">
    Go to **Settings > About Astro** to see your current version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the latest version">
    Download the latest `Astro.dmg` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Blueturboguy07/Astro/releases) or [publikhq.com/astro](https://publikhq.com/astro).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace the app">
    Quit Astro (`Cmd + Q`). Open the disk image and drag **Astro** to your Applications folder. When macOS asks, click **Replace**. Your settings and data stay.
  </Step>
</Steps>
