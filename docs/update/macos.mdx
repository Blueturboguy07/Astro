---
title: "Update on macOS"
description: "How to update Astro on macOS"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your version">
    Go to **Settings > About Astro** to see your current version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for updates">
    Choose **Astro > Check for Updates…** in the menu bar. Astro reads its update feed at publikhq.com and offers a newer version when one exists. Restart Astro to apply a downloaded update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manual download (if needed)">
    If no update is offered, download the latest `Astro.dmg` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Blueturboguy07/Astro/releases) or [publikhq.com/astro](https://publikhq.com/astro), and drag Astro to Applications again. Your settings and data stay.
  </Step>
</Steps>
